# Preamble

The xHain Round Table is an open space in which members discuss openly and decide on equal footing. It is founded on mutual trust and respect, responsibility toward the community, and the aspiration to engage with one another excellently.
It is not an end in itself, and every session has one primary task: to reach a conclusion.

(This is not a place of honor.
It is the duty before all else of every Session of the Round Table to ensure its own end.)

# Sessions

1. The xHain Round Table meets as a rule every second Wednesday from 20:30. Members are duly invited to sessions.
2. The invitation is issued by publishing the motions in the pad no later than the day before the session.
3. Sessions are in principle open to the public.
4. Participation in sessions is also possible online via a video conferencing system (e.g. BigBlueButton). The session chair must enable online participation. Members participating online count toward quorum and have the same voting rights as members present in person.
5. Each session begins in the following order:
   1. Election of the session chair;
   2. Election of the minute-taker;
   3. Introductory round;
   4. Vote on the agenda;
   5. Admission of new keyholders;
   6. Confirmation of the minutes of the preceding session;
   7. Working through the agenda.
6. Sessions end at 22:00 or as soon as the agenda has been fully completed.
7. If the regular session date falls on a public holiday in the state of Berlin, the session is cancelled without replacement.

# Session Chair

1. The session chair must perform the role impartially.
2. The session chair consists of one person.
3. The session chair is elected at the beginning of a session by *Simple Majority*. Until the session chair is elected, the opening of the session and the election itself are conducted by the session chair of the preceding session. If that person is not present, the keyholder present with the longest membership in xHain assumes this role.
   If no person obtains a majority of all *Yes* votes in the first ballot, a second ballot takes place between the two persons with the most votes. In the event of a tie in the second ballot, the decision is made by lot.
4. The session chair opens, conducts, and closes the sessions of the xHain Round Table. They ensure the proper order and conduct of the session. In addition to the members of the xHain Round Table, they have the right to table procedural motions.
5. The session chair maintains a speakers list.
   1. Registration on the speakers list is done by signalling.
   2. The session chair gives preference to speakers who have spoken least often on the current agenda item. In the event of an equal number of contributions, the order of registration decides.
   3. A person already on the outstanding speakers list may not register again. Re-registration is only possible after they have delivered their contribution.
   4. On a procedural motion, a FLINTA\*-speakers list may be established for the current agenda item. In that case the speakers list consists of a FLINTA\* list and an open list; the session chair alternately grants the floor as long as speakers remain on both lists. Assignment to the FLINTA\* or open list is by self-identification of the speakers. The FLINTA\*-speakers list ends when the agenda item closes.
6. Before debate on a motion is opened, the mover(s) receive the floor to introduce their motion. At the close of deliberation, the mover(s) and rapporteur(s) have the right to a brief closing statement.
7. The session chair may interrupt the speakers list:
   1. to answer a question directed immediately at the chair;
   2. for a single immediate correction;
   3. to give mover(s) the opportunity to reply.
8. The session chair has the right to divide a motion at their discretion and have it discussed accordingly. They may limit speaking time and call a speaker to the subject matter or to order. If such a call is not complied with, the session chair may withdraw the floor from the speaker.
   In the event of repeated disruption, the session chair may exclude a participant from the session.
10. The session chair may take the floor at any time on procedural matters.
11. The session chair has the right, with effect on the agenda item currently under consideration, to interpret a provision of these Rules of Procedure or to fill a gap therein.

# Minute-Taking

1. The minute-taker consists of one person and is elected at the beginning of each session by *Simple Majority*.
2. The minutes of sessions are prepared by the minute-taker.
3. The minutes must contain:
   1. The date, start, and end of the session,
   2. the exact wording of motions and resolutions, together with the corresponding voting results where applicable, and
   3. a summary of the course of debate.
4. The minutes of the preceding session are confirmed at the beginning of the next session by the xHain Round Table by *simple majority*. Amendments are permissible.
5. After confirmation by the xHain Round Table, the minutes must be made accessible to the public.
6. If parts of the session were not public, the corresponding parts of the minutes are accessible only to members of the xHain Round Table.

# Agenda

1. The xHain Round Table adopts an agenda for each session.
2. The introductory round is a fixed part of every session and cannot be removed by amendment.
3. At the beginning of the session, the session chair presents a proposed agenda, which is put to a vote. Amendments are permissible.
4. Motions submitted for a session of the xHain Round Table are dealt with in the order of the agenda.
5. Motions not dealt with in a session are automatically deferred to the next session.

# Quorum

1. The xHain Round Table has quorum when at least 10 of its members are present and due notice has been given. Quorum must be established at the beginning of each session.
2. If the session chair determines that quorum is not present, the session must be closed. Up to that point, the xHain Round Table has quorum.

# Resolutions

1. All members of the xHain are entitled to vote.
2. Transfer of votes to other persons is not permitted.
3. The xHain Round Table adopts its resolutions, unless otherwise specified, by *Simple Majority* (see section *Voting Procedure*).
4. Each member of the xHain Round Table generally has one vote, unless otherwise specified.
5. The xHain Round Table votes openly. Secret ballots do not take place.
6. Votes may, on the proposal of the session chair, be held en bloc, provided no member of the xHain Round Table objects.
7. In the event of a tie (*deadlock*), a resolution is deemed not adopted.
8. Where several motions are before the body, the vote is taken first on the more far-reaching motion in terms of substance. Amendments are voted on before the main motion. In cases of doubt, this order is determined by the session chair.

## Voting Procedure

1. The following voting procedures exist:
   1. *Simple Majority*
      Voting is by *Yes*, *No*, and *Abstention*. A vote is deemed adopted if more *Yes* votes than *No* votes are cast.
   2. *Two-Thirds Majority*
      Voting is by *Yes*, *No*, and *Abstention*. A vote is deemed adopted if the number of *Yes* votes amounts to at least two thirds of the sum of *Yes* and *No* votes.
   3. *Fist of Five*
      Each eligible voter simultaneously shows with their hand how strongly they support a proposal.
      - *0 / Fist*: I block — in my view the proposal is currently not viable.
      - *1 finger*: Strong reservations; I see significant risks.
      - *2 fingers*: I have relevant objections; not yet workable as is.
      - *3 fingers*: I can go along, but have open points.
      - *4 fingers*: Good solution; I support it.
      - *5 fingers*: Full agreement and active conviction.
      A vote is deemed adopted if no vote below *3 fingers* is cast.
   4. *Objection*
      A vote is deemed adopted if no eligible voter raises an objection.
2. Unless otherwise specified, the voting procedure is *Simple Majority*.

# Motions

1. Every member of the xHain has the right to table motions in the Round Table.
2. A distinction is made between resolution motions, discussion motions, and amendment motions.
3. Motions must be submitted in writing, unless these Rules of Procedure provide otherwise.
4. Motions must be submitted no later than 2 working days before a session of the xHain Round Table. Submission is effected by entering the motion in the pad for the respective session. Amendment motions are exempt from this deadline; they must be submitted no later than the beginning of consideration of the corresponding motion. Motions that do not meet this deadline will not be considered. Exceptions are not permitted.
5. The mover must be present throughout the entire consideration of the motion, including debate and vote (in person or online pursuant to § Sessions 4). If the mover leaves the session before the vote is concluded, or is not present from the outset, the motion is deferred to the next session.
6. The mover may withdraw the motion at any time up to the opening of the vote. If amendment motions to the motion are pending, withdrawal is only possible up to the opening of the vote on the first amendment motion.

## Form

1. Motions contain in particular:
   1. a title,
   2. the type of motion (resolution motion, discussion motion, or amendment motion),
   3. the name or handle of the mover,
   4. an e-mail address or Matrix nickname,
   5. a description of the subject matter of the motion,
   6. an estimate of the time required to deal with the motion.
2. Any member may leave comments on a motion in the pad. Comments must be identified with the name or handle of the commenting person. They serve for brief statements; more extensive discussion is reserved for the session.
3. Motions without name identification (anonymous) will not be considered.
4. The agenda is publicly accessible. Movers are therefore requested not to include personal data of third parties in the motion.

## Resolution Motions

1. A resolution motion aims at a binding decision by the xHain Round Table.
2. A resolution motion is adopted or rejected by a vote and takes immediate binding effect upon adoption.
3. Resolution motions additionally contain a proposed voting procedure.

## Discussion Motions

1. A discussion motion serves to facilitate structured deliberation on a topic without aiming at a resolution.
2. A discussion motion does not give rise to a binding decision of the xHain Round Table and may request a non-binding indication of sentiment.

## Amendment Motions

1. An amendment motion serves to alter the wording of a resolution motion or discussion motion currently before the session. An amendment motion cannot have independent binding effect; it exclusively modifies the motion to which it refers.
2. Amendment motions additionally contain:
   1. a reference to the motion to be amended (title and mover),
   2. the amended wording of the affected passage in plain text,
   3. a justification for the proposed change.
3. Amendment motions that relate solely to the voting procedure require no written form.
4. Amendment motions are voted on exclusively by *Simple Majority*.
5. Amendment motions may be accepted immediately and without a vote by the mover(s) of the corresponding motion.

# Keyholders

1. Keyholders are members of the xHain who hold a key to the xHain's premises and share responsibility for their use.
2. The xHain Round Table may designate new keyholders. The admission of new keyholders is dealt with immediately after the vote on the agenda.
3. Registration on the keyholder list is carried out by the applying person themselves and requires the endorsement of at least three existing keyholders, which must be documented together with the registration.
4. In all other respects, the deadlines set out in the section *Motions* apply.
5. New keyholders are voted on by *Objection*. Any other voting procedure is excluded in this regard.

# Procedural Motions

1. Any member of the xHain Round Table may table procedural motions. A procedural motion is signalled by raising both hands. The mover receives the floor next. Speeches or procedural motions currently being dealt with may not be interrupted thereby.
2. The procedural motion is followed by at most one counter-speech, signalled by raising both hands. If no counter-speech is made, the procedural motion is deemed adopted without a formal vote. If a counter-speech is made, a vote on the procedural motion by *Simple Majority* takes place immediately thereafter.
3. Only the following motions are to be regarded as procedural motions:
   1. Amendment of the adopted agenda; this does not include the subsequent addition of motions that have not met the submission deadline;
   2. Limitation of speaking time;
   3. Closure of the speakers list;
   4. Closure of debate, possibly with immediate vote;
   5. Reopening of debate;
   6. Non-consideration of a motion;
   7. Deferral of an agenda item;
   8. Referral to a working group or branch of the xHain;
   9. Five-minute session recess (maximum two per session);
   10. Exclusion of the public;
   11. Establishment of quorum;
   12. Closure of the session;
   13. Constructive vote of no confidence in or removal of the session chair;
   14. Establishment of a FLINTA\*-speakers list for the current agenda item.
4. The motion to establish quorum (§3.11) admits no counter-speech and is deemed adopted upon tabling; the session chair establishes quorum without delay.
5. Before the speakers list is closed, every member of the xHain Round Table must be given the opportunity to still register on it.
6. Points of order and enquiries may be raised at any time and must be dealt with immediately like procedural motions.

# Conflicts of Interest

1. A member is considered to have a conflict of interest if they have a direct personal, financial, or other special interest in the matter under deliberation that goes beyond the common interest of the xHain members. A conflict of interest may exist in particular in the case of:
   1. Motions that directly benefit or burden the member themselves, persons close to them, or organisations in which they hold a leading role;
   2. Contracts, assignments, or financial benefits in which the member has a financial interest;
   3. Personnel decisions affecting the member themselves or persons close to them.
2. Whoever considers themselves to have a conflict of interest in a matter, or knows of circumstances that may give rise to the appearance of a conflict of interest in other members, must disclose this before the beginning of deliberation on the relevant agenda item. The disclosure is noted in the minutes.
3. In case of doubt, the existence of a conflict of interest is decided by the xHain Round Table by *Simple Majority* without the participation of the person concerned.
4. A member determined to have a conflict of interest does not participate in the vote on the relevant agenda item. The right to participate in the remainder of the session is not affected.

# Amendment of the Rules of Procedure

1. These Rules of Procedure may be amended by the xHain Round Table.
2. Motions to amend the Rules of Procedure follow the general rules of the section *Motions*. The proposed wording of the amendment must be stated in the motion.
3. Motions to amend these Rules of Procedure are voted on by *Two-Thirds Majority*. Any other voting procedure is excluded in this regard.

# Entry into Force

1. These Rules of Procedure enter into force upon their adoption by the xHain Round Table.

# Appendix: Sample Motions

## Example 1: Resolution Motion

**Title:** Procurement of an Axion Emitter for the xVoid Branch

**Type:** Resolution motion

**Mover:** rook

**E-Mail / Matrix:** rook@xhain.space / @rook:xhain.space

**Time estimate:** 10 minutes

**Voting procedure:** Simple Majority

---

The xVoid Branch requests the procurement of an Axion Emitter (model: CausaLink 9000) at a price of 480 euros.

The device enables superluminal signal transmission within the network and replaces the existing workaround via nested wormhole proxies. The latter are unstable and have disrupted the Wi-Fi several times in recent weeks.

The treasurer notes that general liquidity is tight. A postponement would be conceivable.

rook: The xVoid Branch has sufficient reserves from branch membership fees. No general xHain funds are to be used. However, larger purchases require the approval of the Round Table.

**Type:** Amendment motion

## Example 3: Discussion Motion

**Title:** Usage Policy for the Community Teleporter

**Type:** Discussion motion

**Mover:** Quantenqualle (qqualle)

**E-Mail / Matrix:** qqualle@xhain.space / @qqualle:xhain.space

**Time estimate:** 15 minutes

---

The xHain's goal is to promote creative engagement with technology. Recently, the community teleporter has been increasingly used for private individual transports, e.g. personal errands.
These uses are not community-oriented and monopolise a device that is actually intended for collaborative projects (e.g. meetups, material deliveries, etc.).

I also see potential for conflict regarding who has stored the teleporter profile and who may use which coordinates and when.

I would like to discuss whether we need a usage policy for individual transports.

My personal assessment: group beaming is less problematic, as it promotes social interaction and distributes resource use across several people.

**Comments:**

- vera (@vera): Fully agree. Last week I waited 20 minutes because someone beamed to the supermarket alone.

- fluxusflux (@fluxusflux): Maybe just a booking system? Didn't we already discuss that for the laser cutter?
